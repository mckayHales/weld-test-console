## 2. Possible differences from D1.1:2020

**Basis.** The 2025 side of each comparison comes from the uploaded pages. The 2020 side comes from Claude's recollection of the 2020 edition, not an uploaded copy. Confidence is marked on each item. Confirm in the 2020 book before you rely on any of these. **In code, search for 2020 table numbers and update them.**

### High confidence: supported by the 2025 TOC itself

1. **Welder qualification tables are split between plate and pipe.**
   - In 2025, plate tests are in Clause 6 (Tables 6.11 and 6.12). Pipe and box tube tests are in Clause 10 (Table 10.8, Table 10.12 and 10.16).
   - The new title words are underlined in the TOC.
   - In 2020, one pair of Clause 6 tables covered plate, pipe and box tube together. From recollection these were Tables 6.10 and 6.11.
   - **Weld Sync impact:** pipe and tube coupons need their ranges from Clause 10 tables, not Clause 6.

2. **New Table 6.5 (plug and slot WPS qualification) renumbered Tables 6.6–6.17.** Every Clause 6 table after 6.5 sits one number higher than in 2020. For example:
   - The PQR essential-variable table is now **6.6**. From recollection it was 6.5 in 2020.
   - The welder essential-variable table is now **6.13**. From recollection it was 6.12.
   - Electrode Classification Groups is now **6.14**. From recollection it was 6.13.

3. **Clause 5 tables were reorganized.** Confirmed from the Clause 5 pages:
   - Prequalified WPS requirements are split by process in **Tables 5.1–5.4**.
   - Essential variables are in **Table 5.5**.
   - Approved base metals are in **Table 5.6** (2020: Table 5.3, from recollection).
   - Filler metals are in **Table 5.7**.
   - Preheat is in **Table 5.11**.

4. **New or renumbered Clause 6 figures.** Figures 6.15 and 6.17–6.31 are underlined. They include:
   - a new single performance test coupon figure, Figure 6.21
   - plug/slot coupons
   - consumables verification
   - fillet test options 1–3

5. **Clause 10 tables and figures renumbered.** Table numbers 10.13–10.17 and figure numbers 10.18–10.29 are underlined. Figure 10.17 now covers WPS qualification as well as welder qualification.

6. **Annex letters revised.** The letters for Annexes I, J and M–T are underlined in the TOC. Notes:
   - Annex C does not appear in the TOC.
   - Annex J is now Sample Welding Forms.
   - Annex K is Contents of Prequalified WPS.
   - Annex O is the UT forms.

   Don't carry 2020 annex references forward.

### Lower confidence: check against the 2020 book
7. 6.14 Plug and Slot Welds and 6.15 Consumable Verification Test may be new subclauses, or renumbered ones.
8. Clause 10 now has its own Part D (Performance Qualification) and Part F (Inspection) with Table 10.14 for tubular visual acceptance. I can't confirm which subclauses moved.
9. "F-numbers": D1.1:2025 groups SMAW electrodes in Table 6.14, "Electrode Classification Groups".
