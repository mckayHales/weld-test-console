## 2. Acceptance criteria

### 2a. Nontubular (Table 8.1)

| (1) Cracks | None, of any size or location | Same |
| (2) Fusion | Complete between layers and to the base metal | Same |
| (3) Craters | Filled to the specified size, except at the ends of intermittent fillets outside their effective length | Same |
| (4) Profiles | Per 7.23. See 2c below. | Same |
| (5) Time of inspection | After cooling to ambient. For A514, A517 and A709 HPS 100W [690W], inspect at least 48 h after completion. | Same |
| (6) Undersized fillets | Allowed underrun below nominal size S: up to 1/16 in for S of 1/8–3/16 in; up to 3/32 in for S = 1/4 in; up to 1/8 in for S ≥ 5/16 in. The undersize may not exceed 10% of the weld length. No underrun at girder web-to-flange ends for 2× the flange width. | Same |
| (7) Undercut | Material < 1 in: ≤ 1/32 in. Welds ≥ 12 in long may have up to 1/16 in over an accumulated 2 in in any 12 in. Welds < 12 in: see the [VERIFY] note below. ### 2c. Weld profiles (7.23 → Table 7.8, Table 7.9, Fig. 7.4)

**General rules (7.23)**
- **Fillets (7.23.1):** profile per Fig. 7.4. The convexity limit applies to the whole face width **and** to each individual surface bead of a multipass weld.
- **Intermittent fillets (7.23.2):** the Fig. 7.4 profile limits don't apply at the ends outside the effective length, but **undercut limits apply over the full length**.
- **Groove welds (7.23.3):** reinforcement per Tables 7.8 and 7.9, with a gradual transition to the base metal.
- **Flush welds (7.23.3.1):** may not reduce the thinner base or weld metal by more than 1/32 in. Remaining reinforcement ≤ 1/32 in, blended smoothly, no undercut. All reinforcement must be removed on faying or contact surfaces.
- **Finish (7.23.3.2):** where finishing is required, roughness ≤ 250 µin. On cyclically loaded structures, finish parallel to the primary stress unless the roughness is ≤ 125 µin.
- **Shelf bars (7.23.4):** per 7.9.1.1–7.9.1.5. They may be left in place only on statically loaded members.

**Which schedule applies (Table 7.8)**

| Weld | Butt | Corner, inside | Corner, outside | T-joint | Lap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Groove (CJP/PJP) | Fig. 7.4A, Schedule A | Fig. 7.4B, Schedule B | Fig. 7.4C, Schedule A | Fig. 7.4D, Schedule B | N/A |
| Fillet | N/A | Fig. 7.4E, Schedule C | Fig. 7.4F, Schedule C or D | Fig. 7.4E, Schedule C | Fig. 7.4E, Schedule C |

- For a butt joint with a shelf bar, use Fig. 7.4G. Footnote c exempts shelf-bar welds, and horizontal welds between vertical bars of unequal thickness, from the R and C limits.
- Footnote b: where reinforcing fillets are required by design, the limits apply to the groove and the fillet separately.

**Schedules (Table 7.9)**

| Schedule | What t or W means | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| A (reinforcement R) | t = thicker plate (CJP), or the weld size (PJP) | R min 0. R max: 1/8 in (t ≤ 1 in); 3/16 in (1 < t ≤ 2 in); 1/4 in (t > 2 in). **Cyclically loaded with t > 2 in: 3/16 in** (footnote a). |
| B | Same as A | R unlimited. Convexity or concavity C max: 1/8 in (t < 1 in); 3/16 in (t ≥ 1 in). |
| C (fillet convexity) | W = width of the weld face or of an individual surface bead | C max: 1/16 in (W ≤ 5/16 in); 1/8 in (5/16 < W < 1 in); 3/16 in (W ≥ 1 in) |
| D | t = thinner exposed edge | C max t/2 |

Footnote b: concavity is not restricted as long as the minimum weld size (leg and throat) is achieved.

**Fig. 7.4A and B:** reinforcement over R_max is unacceptable. An underfilled butt face is also unacceptable.

### 2d. Repairs (7.25)
- Remove material by machining, grinding, chipping or gouging, without nicking adjacent metal.
- **Oxygen gouging only on as-rolled steels.**
- Remaining detail is on p. 209 [GAP].

### 2e. Tubular fit-up and backing
- **Girth-seam offset (10.23.1):** ≤ 0.2t, with a 1/4 in maximum; any offset over 1/8 in must be welded from both sides. One local area up to 0.3t (max 3/8 in, under 8t long) is allowed with the Engineer's approval, with a 4:1 transition.
- **Girth weld spacing:** at least 1 pipe diameter or 3 ft apart, whichever is less, and no more than two in any 10 ft.
- **Longitudinal seams:** staggered at least 90°.
- **Backing (10.22.1):** steel backing is continuous for the full length, with CJP splices. The statically loaded HSS-end exceptions are listed in (1)–(6).

## 3. Report content

### CODE-driven
1. The inspector's role: Contractor's or verification (8.1.3).
2. The drawings and revision inspected against, and whether all welds are shown on them (8.1.6).
3. Each joint or member inspected and accepted (8.5.4), with measured size and length (8.5.1, 8.5.3).
4. Any **unspecified welds**, with the Engineer's approval reference (8.5.1).
5. The WPS(s) in use (8.3.3) and the welders, with their qualification records (8.4.1).
6. The acceptance criteria applied: Table 8.1 static, Table 8.1 cyclic, or Table 10.14 (8.9). Cite any Engineer-approved alternates (8.8).
7. For A514, A517 or A709 HPS 100W: the time of inspection, at least 48 h after completion (Table 8.1(5)).
8. Rejected items and the correction requested of the Contractor (8.6.2).

### BP
- Base metal spec.
- Inspection date distinct from the report date.
- Inspector's eye-exam date.
- Photos.
- List only the codes the contract invokes.

## 4. UT equipment (Table 8.8, p. 253; for future UT reports)

**Equipment qualification**
- Horizontal linearity and gain/dB accuracy: every 2 months.
- Internal reflections: every 40 h of use, per search unit.
- Angle-beam index and angle: every 8 h of use.
- Resolution: before initial use of each instrument and search-unit combination.

**Calibration for testing**
- Range and sensitivity, straight and angle beam, plus index point and angle: just before and at the location of the first weld tested.
- Recalibration every 2 h, and whenever the circuitry is disturbed: transducer, battery, outlet or coax change, or a power outage.

## 6. Tubular positions and RT (also used by the WQTR checklist)

- **Fig. 10.12, groove positions:**
  - 1G rotated: ±15°, filler deposited at or near the top
  - 2G: vertical tube, ±15°
  - 5G: horizontal fixed, ±15°
  - 6G: 45° ± 5° fixed
  - 6GR: 45° ± 5° with a restriction ring, for T-, Y- and K-connections
- **Fig. 10.13, fillet positions:** 1F rotated at 45°, 2F fixed, 2FR rotated, 4F fixed, 5F fixed.
- **10.21.2:** coupons welded in 5G, 6G or 6GR are radiographed from top centerline to bottom centerline on either side. Test pipe under 4 in diameter requires **100% RT**.
